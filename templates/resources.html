Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who is eligible for the Green Scholarship?

Students enrolled in accredited colleges across Karnataka with valid academic records and active participation in environmental activities (tree plantation, recycling, volunteer hours) are eligible to apply.

How is the Green Score calculated?

The Green Score is calculated using a weighted formula based on Trees Planted (3x), Volunteer Hours (1.5x), Recycling Drives (5x), and Academic Percentage (5x).

What documents are required for application?

You need a valid Student ID, College Bonafide Certificate, Academic Marksheet, Activity Verification Proof (Signed by NSS/College Coordinator), and Family Income Certificate.
